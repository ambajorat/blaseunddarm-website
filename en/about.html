About me

I build this app because I need it myself.

My name is André M. Bajorat. I live with a spinal cord injury, and since then bladder and bowel management has been part of my day the way brushing your teeth is for others — only with fixed times.

How it came about

You leave rehab with a plan: every few hours, fixed times, and please write it down. That sounds simple until the first ordinary day gets in the way — a meeting overruns, a train is cancelled, and suddenly you are doing arithmetic in your head about when it last happened.

I tried paper, the Notes app, calendar reminders. None of it lasted longer than two weeks. The apps I found were either built for something else or wanted an account, a server and my data. On a subject I do not even like talking about, that was not an option.

So I started building something for myself. What came of it is now on the App Store — not because it is meant to become a business, but because I suspect others are in the same position.

Who works on it

Me, in the evenings and at weekends. There is no company behind it, no team, no investor. That has drawbacks — some things take a while — and one advantage: when you write to me, it reaches me directly and not a ticketing system.

A good part of what the app can do today came from messages like that. The Bristol scale with symbols instead of bare numbers, for instance, or the quiet hours so that nothing beeps at night.

So there is no misunderstanding

What the app is not.

  • Not a medical device. It documents; it does not diagnose.
  • Not a substitute for medical advice, but a preparation for it.
  • Not a data collection. There is no server holding your entries.
  • No ads, no trackers, nothing sold on to anyone.
